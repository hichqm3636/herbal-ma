# ADR-006: Require Human Approval for AI-Assisted Publishing

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

AI can improve editorial efficiency, but Herbal.ma covers health-related subjects where unsupported, overstated, or context-free claims can cause harm. Automated generation is not evidence or editorial accountability.

## Decision

Permit AI assistance in research organization, drafting, translation, metadata, and review support, but require an accountable human to approve the exact content version before publication. Automation may never bypass the risk-based editorial workflow.

## Consequences

- Content lifecycle state must distinguish generated or drafted content from reviewed, approved, and published content.
- AI cannot be the author, evidence source, final reviewer, or publication authority.
- Arabic and English versions require their own human approval.
- Fully autonomous health-content publishing is prohibited.

## Alternatives Considered

- Fully automated publishing: rejected because it removes accountable health-content review.
- Prohibit all AI assistance: rejected because controlled assistance can improve efficiency without transferring approval authority.
